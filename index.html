Features

One companion, a few good habits

Ari gives your browsing data a shape and a voice.

Home tab

Today at a Glance

See where your hours went across sites and categories, then set a daily intention to keep it that way.

Nudges tab

Gentle Check-ins

Ari nudges you when you start to drift, and keeps a feed of recent check-ins so you can see the pattern, not just the moment.

You tab

Streaks & Milestones

Your streak, sharpest day, peak focus window, and the achievements you've unlocked along the way, all in one glance.

Settings tab

Make It Yours

Set site limits, tune how often Ari nudges you, switch to dark mode, and sign in to keep more than a week of history.

How it works

Up and running in 60 seconds

No setup. Install and Ari is ready to go immediately.

  1. Install in one click

    Add Ari to Chrome from the Web Store and it opens right in your sidebar.

  2. Browse naturally

    Ari quietly tracks your time in the background, without interrupting your regular browsing.

  3. Open the sidebar

    Anytime you want: check your stats or set a goal. Your data is always there, always yours.

Smart categorization

Your data stays local

Time tracking, streaks, category breakdowns, and every stat Ari shows you are computed and stored fully on your device. Ari does not store your browsing data, it stays on your device — nothing about your browsing is ever sent to our servers.

  • Stored in chrome.storage.local, on your machine
  • Your browsing history is never uploaded, synced, or seen by us
  • Delete everything from inside the extension, anytime
