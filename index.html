Your data stays local

Ari's core features, time tracking, streaks, and category breakdowns work fully offline and live in your browser's local storage. The one exception is chat: sending a message shares a short activity summary to generate a reply, and that data isn't stored on our servers afterward.

  • Stored in chrome.storage.local, on your machine
  • Chat sends what's needed for a reply, then it's gone, not stored on our servers
  • Delete everything from inside the extension, anytime
