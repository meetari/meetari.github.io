Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Ari is a Chrome extension that helps you understand where your browsing time goes. This policy explains what data Ari collects, when, and why — and it's split into two parts, because Ari works two different ways depending on whether you create an account.

1. Using Ari without an account

By default, Ari needs no account and no sign-up. Your browsing activity, time tracking, streaks, and category breakdowns are computed and stored entirely in chrome.storage.local — on your own device. None of it is transmitted to our servers, and we have no visibility into it. Uninstalling the extension or clearing its storage deletes it for good.

2. Using an Ari Account

Signing in (to chat with Ari or subscribe to Pro) is optional. It requires an email address and involves the following:

Account & sign-in

  • Your email address, used to send a one-time sign-in code. We don't use passwords.
  • An account identifier and, optionally, a display name you provide.

Chat with Ari

  • Your messages, a short recent history, and a same-day activity summary the extension builds on your device (categories and minutes, not your raw browsing history) are sent with each chat request to generate a reply.
  • Chat messages themselves are not stored on our servers beyond what's needed to serve the request; we do keep a count of tokens used, to enforce usage limits.

Weekly summaries

  • If you use the weekly summary feature, the aggregated stats behind it (total time, streaks, top categories) are sent to our servers to generate a written summary, and the summary itself is stored so it doesn't need to be regenerated. This feature is not currently sending emails automatically to anyone; this section describes what happens if and when it's turned back on.

Subscriptions & payments

  • Ari Pro is billed through Paddle, our payment processor. Paddle collects and processes your payment details directly — we never receive or store your full card number.
  • We keep a record of your subscription status and credit balance to enforce plan limits.

Feedback

  • If you submit the feedback form, we store what you write (the category, title, and details) and your email address if you choose to provide one, so we can read it and reply if needed.

3. Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run Ari. They only receive what's needed to do their job:

  • Cloudflare — hosts our servers and database.
  • Paddle — processes payments and manages subscriptions.
  • Replicate — powers the AI behind chat and your weekly write-up.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

4. Data retention

We keep account data for as long as your account is active. Feedback submissions may be retained longer to help us track and act on recurring issues.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of your account data, ask us to delete it, or close your account entirely at any time by emailing us. If you'd rather not share any data with us at all, simply don't create an account — Ari's core time-tracking features work fully offline.

6. Children's privacy

Ari isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests about your data? Email us at hello@ari.app.
